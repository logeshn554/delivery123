Authentication issue: 64-character code
server/accounts.mjs generates an email verification token using randomBytes(32).toString('hex'). VerifyAccount.tsx requires the resulting 64-character token in an input field. Password recovery in Neon mode follows a similar manual-code approach.
This is technically workable through copy-and-paste, but unnecessarily difficult for customers switching between an email app and AllGo.
Replace the manual long-token experience with a secure clickable verification link, or a shorter rate-limited one-time code. Keep expiration, attempt limits and server-side verification.

7. Customer acceptance criteria
Before I would consider AllGo reliable as a customer, I would expect every service to satisfy these conditions:

7. I can report an incorrect, missing or damaged item without explaining the entire case in free text.

Final verdict
The strongest part of the current AllGo codebase is that it already has substantial customer screens, service quotes, protected bookings, payment recovery and order lifecycle code.
The weakest part is still the experience after something unexpected happens: no driver, delayed food, unavailable groceries, damaged parcels, vehicle disputes, payment uncertainty and unresolved support requests.
Build those recovery journeys before adding new services. A consumer will forgive a missing animation. They will not forgive a paid order that cannot be completed or resolved.
